CLASH OF CODERS

Eligibility & Event Details

Eligibility: All 2nd and 3rd year students from Panimalar Engineering College, across all departments.

Date: 11th & 13th August

Venue: CC20 or CC21

Official Contest Guidelines

General Instructions

  • All participants must possess an active HackerRank account prior to the contest.
  • Teams must strictly adhere to all rules and round-specific regulations.
  • Any team violating contest guidelines will be immediately disqualified.
  • The use of AI tools, automated solvers, or external coding assistance is strictly prohibited.
  • Discussion is permitted only within registered team members.
  • Decisions made by the organizing committee will be final and binding.

Round 0 – Codefront: The Battle Begins (Online)

  • Each land has a fixed entry limit (first-come, first-served).
  • A team can participate in a maximum of two lands.
  • Only one member per team per land.
  • Highest scorer conquers the land.
  • Each team conquers strictly one land at end of Round 0.

Round 1 – Code Warfare: The Strategic Conquest (Offline)

Attacking Phase

  • Teams may attempt to conquer new lands.
  • Each land can be attacked by only one member from a team.
  • Multiple team members may attack different lands simultaneously.

Defending Phase

  • Teams must defend previously conquered lands.
  • All four members may participate in defense.
  • Strict time limit will be provided.
  • Top teams with highest conquered lands advance.
